# Quickstart QA

1. Open Today on Sunday and enter Strain: Sunday is the seventh M-S slot and is highlighted.
2. Navigate Today to a historical day with gaps and open Strain/Recovery: title, hero, summaries, contributors, and history share that day.
3. Open Trends: confirm the current-week range is Monday-Sunday.
4. Switch W/M/3M/6M: the range chart retains calendar spacing and “Average by weekday” updates to 7/30/90/180 days.
5. Scrub a point after a gap: the tooltip names the point's real date.
