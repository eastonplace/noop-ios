Opens the load chart, daily effort and how it is calculated
Both lines are running averages of your daily Effort. Each new day pulls the line toward it and older days fade gradually, so recent days count most.
Acute fades over about a week and reacts quickly. Chronic fades over about six weeks and moves slowly. The range you pick changes what is drawn, never these numbers.
Balance is chronic minus acute. Above zero, recent effort is lighter than your longer-term level; below zero, it is heavier.
The first seven recorded days are averaged to start both lines, which begin on the seventh. Load appears after 14 recorded days and is marked Established at 42.
After the first seven days, up to two missing days in a row count as zero Effort and are not drawn. A longer gap starts the history over. A recorded zero is a real rest day.
