## Bounded follow-up contracts

- **Live sleep:** `AppModel.sleepMarks` are explicitly unpaired marks. Repository sleep sessions are saved windows; beta band sleep-state samples are historical observations. Neither supplies a qualified current asleep/awake contract. `NotificationCoordinator.productionContext` therefore supplies unknown sleep. The pure policy rejects proactive coaching with `sleep_state_unavailable`. In-app results, chosen reminders and existing device choices remain available. A known asleep observation also suppresses proactive notices. Default quiet hours are a timing control, not sleep detection.
- **Charge reminder action:** Only the production `chargeReminder.battery` family offers one 15-minute Snooze. It requires the accepted original, its active physical-device charge episode, current source/preferences/permission and a useful time before the original 30-minute expiry. It uses a durable distinct user-requested occurrence; a repeated action cannot reset its identity or retry allowance. The reminder has no further Snooze action. Measured recovery cancels both requests. Other launch families retain Open and Turn off; Snooze for recurring sleep/review and saved workout/journal windows remains deferred.
- **Producer fixtures:** Tests cover combined low/runtime evidence, process replacement, unavailable and invalid runtime, charging-bit changes, measured recovery, percentage-only rearming, independent devices, full-charge withdrawal, original-expiry retention, source changes, accepted-original requirements, repeated actions and bounded known-failure retries. Shared policy budgets still apply to a newly rearmed device episode.
- **Await boundaries:** Per-device observation tokens stop an older battery producer from submitting after a newer observation. Submission rechecks current charging separately from durable episode identity. Accepted and accepted-then-uncertain adds share source, preference, permission, privacy and episode cleanup. Known cancellation before `add` clears its unused budget; uncertain outcomes retain consumed identity and attention.
- **Journal completion:** The adapter uses `JournalCatalogStore.resolvedItems` and persisted Active coaching memberships. No active visible questions means no reminder. Every selected current-day native row must be present; explicit No and finite numeric zero count as answered. Partial answers remain eligible. Imported rows and another civil day cannot complete the entry. Completion cancels the same owned pending request. There is no journal-wide Skip state; a stack's Skip applies only to that stack. Catalog and membership edits trigger reconciliation after persistence.
- **Recovery:** Merging recovery code does not open the literal false adapter gate. A later reviewed change must qualify exact wake identity, measurement freshness, source/model acceptance and sleep context before phone coaching can activate.
- **Scenario expiry:** Recovered catalog WT021/024/027/030 require the chosen workout start plus 120 minutes; HC002 requires the chosen journal time plus 120 minutes. The planner preserves these limits, including a workout's separate reminder lead. `NotificationScheduleTests.testWorkoutExpiryUsesChosenStartRatherThanReminderLeadAndJournalUsesTwoHours` covers the deadlines and policy rejection at expiry. OS presentation remains outside the app's deadline guarantee.
- **Migration:** The production store must register recovery v60 before notification v61 after Lift strain v59. The integrated migration tests and a data-preserving upgrade must execute on the final release head; synthetic table fixtures are not sufficient evidence.

## Evidence limits and remaining gates

The supplied original design archive has been recovered. Its 540-row catalog, separate failure matrix, policy and validation artifacts are retained under `handoff/`; the scenario coverage map records implementation scope separately. Artifact validation checks the design library. It does not prove that all catalog rows are production rules or that every failure scenario passed in the app. Later P2/P3 families remain backlog.

This source pass did not run Swift package tests, Xcode builds, iOS tests, Simulator or phone operations. The integration owner must record those results separately. Required rendered and device checks include normal/accessibility Settings, read-only destinations, privacy, Focus, cold launch, permission changes, saved schedule edits and cancellation, timezone travel, restarts, reconnects, preserved wake alarms and a real settled Lift completion. Pending OS requests prove acceptance only; they do not prove presentation or delivery timing.
